# MORI project instructions

- For MORI work involving campus mental-health support, help-seeking guidance, urgency recognition, crisis escalation, privacy, resource routing, knowledge-base curation, or acceptance testing, use the global `campus-wellbeing-resource-guide` skill first and follow it completely.
- Keep MORI framed as a psychological resource navigator and help guide, not a counselor, therapist, doctor, diagnostic system, or emergency service.
- Use only verified school and public resource information. Never invent contact details, opening hours, response times, confidentiality promises, or emergency capabilities.
- Do not describe MORI as production-ready until the institutional resources, emergency workflow, privacy notice, safeguarding rules, human ownership, and update schedule required by the global skill have been verified.
